Welcome, judges. This is FinWise — Personal Finance, Intelligently Yours. We built FinWise because the personal finance tools most people use today are passive dashboards. They show you what happened. They don't tell you what to do next, and they certainly don't do it for you. FinWise is different. It is an agentic AI copilot: a system that reasons about your money, takes safe actions on your behalf through natural language, and gives you a clearer view of your financial future than any spreadsheet ever could. Over the next ten minutes I'll walk you through the problem we're solving, our four-layer architecture, the eleven tools our agent can call, our advanced analytics, five novel innovation features, our security model, and the live demo. Let's get into it.
