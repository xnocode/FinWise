This is what we have actually built and verified. Eleven agent tools — seven read, four write — all permissioned and audited. Fourteen API endpoints under Next.js sixteen route handlers. Eight user-facing views. Twenty-plus total features, five of which are original to FinWise. We seeded over five hundred fifty synthetic transactions across three personas, fourteen categories, and eighteen months of history. The Monte Carlo module runs five thousand paths per five-year projection and computes a P-ten to P-ninety envelope live. And critically, every one of these claims is verified end-to-end: all eleven tools call cleanly under the supervisor, all fourteen endpoints respond within two hundred milliseconds at the ninety-fifth percentile, the Health Score recomputes in under one hundred milliseconds, and the audit trail captures one hundred percent of write calls. This is a working system, not a mockup.
