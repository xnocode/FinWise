Thank you. We built FinWise to close the gap between knowing what to do with your money and actually doing it. Today's apps are dashboards; FinWise is a copilot. The agent is live, the analytics are real, the audit trail is complete, and the demo is yours to drive. Over the next ninety days we have three milestones. In the next thirty days we add Plaid bank connectivity to replace synthetic seed data with real-time ingest. At sixty days we ship a React Native mobile companion sharing the same agent and ledger, with voice input for on-the-go logging. At ninety days we open a two-hundred-user beta cohort with privacy-preserving analytics to iterate on trigger detection. The team is a small group of full-stack, AI, and design builders. The live demo is at finwise.app/demo and the source is at github.com/finwise. We'd love your questions.
