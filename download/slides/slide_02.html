Let's start with the problem. Personal finance is a domain where the tools are abundant but the outcomes are poor. Seventy percent of Americans live paycheck to paycheck — that includes many in higher income brackets, which tells you this is not an income problem, it's a management problem. Seventy-two percent of adults report money as a primary stressor, yet only thirty-two percent maintain any kind of household budget. The gap between intention and execution is enormous. Why? Because today's apps are passive. They are dashboards that show you what already happened, then leave you to do the hard part: categorize correctly, forecast the future, choose between competing goals, and actually follow through. FinWise closes that gap by making the agent an active participant in your financial life.
