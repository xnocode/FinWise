FinWise · Topic A03 / 12
Our solution is structural, not cosmetic. We didn't bolt a chat box onto a budgeting app. We built a four-layer finance engine — ingestion, categorization, agent, insight — and placed an LLM supervisor at the top. The supervisor understands intent and routes to worker agents that call one of eleven verified tools. Seven tools are read-only: they query transactions, goals, budgets, and analytics. Four are write tools: they add transactions, create goals, update budgets, and so on — every one of them inside a permissioned, audited sandbox. The right-hand panel is the simplest way to understand the shift. In the old world you typed transactions into a form. In FinWise you just say "I spent forty-two dollars at Trader Joe's." The agent parses, categorizes, persists, and updates the affected budget envelope — automatically.
