FinWise · Topic A10 / 12
We seeded three personas across five hundred fifty-plus synthetic transactions, and we demo each one end-to-end live. Maya is a college senior and part-time barista with tight runway and competing goals — grad school and a summer trip. In one utterance she splits her paycheck between them, and the agent creates two goals, sets contribution schedules, and flags a twelve-day cash shortfall predicted by Holt-Winters. Devin is a freelancer with lumpy income and a looming tax bill. He uses the What-If builder to simulate a thirty-day client delay and Monte Carlo to see his P-ten runway drop to eighteen days. The Chen family has two earners, two kids, and three competing goals: college fund, emergency reserve, and a kitchen renovation. The Goal Collision Detector finds a one hundred sixty-two dollar monthly gap, and the three-tier permission model lets parents be Approvers while their teenager is a Viewer.
