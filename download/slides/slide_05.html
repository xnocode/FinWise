FinWise · Topic A05 / 12
FinWise ships eight user-facing views organized into four pillars. Smart budgeting auto-builds envelopes from your transaction history and flags category drift before it becomes a problem. Bill analysis detects recurring payments from merchant cadence — the average FinWise user is surprised to find around twelve hundred dollars a month across nine subscriptions they had forgotten about. Spending insights pair a 365-day heatmap with merchant-concentration analysis using the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index, plus seasonal decomposition. Actionable recommendations close the loop: the goal optimizer, debt-payoff planner, and What-If simulator don't just suggest — they offer one-utterance execution. The bottom card shows the analytics pillar: Holt-Winters forecasting, five-year Monte Carlo with five thousand paths, and the Financial Health Score that blends savings rate, debt ratio, emergency runway, and consistency into a single number.
