FinWise · Topic A09 / 12
An agentic system that can write to your financial life has to be trustworthy by design. We built three layers of defense. First, a three-tier permission model: Viewers can read, Editors can read and write, Approvers additionally grant permissions and review the audit trail. Every tool call is checked at the boundary — there is no privilege escalation path. Second, privacy mode masks sensitive balances, merchant names, and goal amounts in one toggle, default-on for shared screens. Third, every write tool call is logged with actor, tool, payload diff, and timestamp, in a searchable, exportable audit trail shown live on the right. On compliance: FinWise supports GDPR right-to-access, rectify, and erase; CCPA do-not-sell and data portability; and PIPL consent and cross-border transfer disclosure. The risk register tracks our top three mitigations, including hallucinated writes, privilege escalation, and PII in LLM context.
